Acme Payments — Operations Handbook

Teams

The Payments team owns the checkout and settlement services. Priya Nair leads Payments and is the primary on-call escalation for settlement incidents.

The Platform team owns the shared datastore and the internal message bus. Marco Rossi leads Platform.

People

Priya Nair is a staff engineer on the Payments team. She is accountable for the checkout service and is first on the settlement on-call rotation.

Sam Okafor is a senior engineer on the Payments team and maintains the settlement service.

Marco Rossi is the Platform team lead and owns the datastore service.

Services

The checkout service accepts customer orders. It depends on the settlement service to capture funds and on the datastore to persist orders.

The settlement service captures funds from the payment processor. It depends on the datastore and publishes settlement events to the message bus.

The datastore service is the shared Postgres cluster owned by Platform. Losing the datastore takes down both checkout and settlement.

The message bus distributes settlement events to downstream consumers.

Operational policy

Policy: every customer-facing service must have a named on-call owner. Checkout and settlement on-call escalates to Priya Nair.

Policy: changes to the datastore require sign-off from the Platform lead because checkout and settlement both depend on it.
