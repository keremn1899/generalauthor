# Master services agreement

Counterparty: Northbridge Analytics, Inc.
Effective: 4 June 2021
Term: 24 months. The agreement auto-renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 90 days' written notice before the then-current term ends.

## 12. Change of control

Either party may terminate this agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party undergoes a change of control.

This agreement does not require consent or notice as a condition of assignment, except that assignment to a direct competitor is prohibited.

## 19. Governing law

Delaware.
