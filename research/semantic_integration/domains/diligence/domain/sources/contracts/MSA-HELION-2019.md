# Master services agreement

Counterparty: Helion Robotics Ltd.
Effective: 12 March 2019
Term: 36 months, then successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.

## 14. Assignment

Helion Robotics Ltd. may not assign this agreement without prior written notice to the provider. Notice is required; consent is not required for assignment to an affiliate.

## 18. Change of control

If a change of control of Helion Robotics Ltd. occurs, Helion shall obtain the provider's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 21. Governing law

England and Wales.
