# Logistics services agreement

Counterparty: Oakfield Logistics GmbH
Effective: 1 September 2018
Term: rolling 12-month periods.

## 8. Exclusivity

During the term, the provider is the exclusive warehousing contractor for the counterparty's German inbound freight.

## 11. Assignment

Oakfield Logistics GmbH shall not assign this agreement without the provider's prior written consent.

## 16. Change of control

This agreement is silent on change of control.

## 20. Governing law

Germany.
