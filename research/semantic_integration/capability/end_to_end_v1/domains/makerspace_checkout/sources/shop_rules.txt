Shop rules — Eastside Makerspace (posted 20 June 2024)

Laser cutter use requires certification LASER-1. A checkout without that certification is not an authorized use.

CNC mill use requires certification CNC-1.

After 21:00 local time, hourly_fee is multiplied by 1.5.

Note HOLD means the tool was parked in the bay. It is not an extra billed day.

This posting does not define other checkout notes.
