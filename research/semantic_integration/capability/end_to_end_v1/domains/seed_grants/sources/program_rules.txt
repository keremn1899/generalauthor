Municipal Seed Grants — program rules (FY2024 excerpt)

SEED-CORE awards require a cash match equal to twenty percent (20%) of the award amount unless a written waiver is on file with the grants office.

SEED-FAST awards have no cash-match requirement.

Remaining obligation equals the award amount minus the sum of recorded disbursements for that award.

This excerpt does not attach waiver letters. Absence of a waiver in this packet is not a denial that a waiver exists elsewhere.
