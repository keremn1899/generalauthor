# World IR constructor design

This directory does not copy or rewrite the long World IR constructor design.

It exists so experimental status cannot drift away from that design. The
evidence table is in `EVIDENCE_STATUS.md`.

The experimental kernel that currently exists in this repository is
`research/semantic_integration/`. It wraps TaskView. It is not a production
World IR implementation, a universal ontology, a provider framework, or a
general constructor.

Do not treat success on the tiny BOM family as arbitrary-domain
generalization or economic amortization.
